How the numbers connect

Prefermented flour is a share of all flour. A preferment’s hydration is its own water ÷ its own flour. Ripe levain weight is flour + water, not another ingredient to add on top of the overall formula. These columns allocate ripe preferment composition; they are not fresh-feed recipes.

03Set aside the preferments

Required ripe quantities. Transfer each once into the final dough.

The exercise does not specify levain seed quantity, seed hydration or a complete build schedule. These are composition allocations, not instructions to mix fresh flour and water without a culture. Use your established build to obtain the ripe quantity shown.

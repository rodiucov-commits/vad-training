      Composition & formula-linking notes

      The original exercises supply ripe-preferment composition but no seed amount/hydration. This adaptation explicitly decomposes the editable starter and shows fresh-feed additions. No fermentation schedule is inferred from the demonstration starter dose. Trace sponge yeast is unquantified in the source and excluded here; instant yeast is 0.4% of total flour. Formula and portion edits are untested variations.

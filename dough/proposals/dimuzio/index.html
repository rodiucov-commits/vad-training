Composition & formula-linking notes

These are ripe-preferment composition allocations, not fresh-feed recipes. Seed amount, seed hydration and a complete levain build schedule are not supplied by these exercises. Obtain the ripe quantities with your established build. Trace sponge yeast is unquantified in the source and excluded here; instant yeast is 0.4% of total flour. Formula and portion edits are untested variations.

Source: Daniel T. DiMuzio, Bread Baking: An Artisan’s Perspective (2010), printed pp. 36–38, 165–169. Independently recalculated proposal. Live recipes unchanged.

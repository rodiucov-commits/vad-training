# Suas sourdough culture defects

Reference utility under Calculators, not a numerical culture diagnostic or an editable recipe record. Source: Michel Suas, *Advanced Bread and Pastry*, first edition (2009), printed p98/PDF112, Fig4-13. Original180dpi page image inspected for column/row boundaries and all examples. The unrelated Fig4-14 feeding/levain formula below it is not imported. Book master unchanged.

## Transcription scope

Four original groups remain distinct:

1. Low acidity / lack of strength / bland bread:4 causes,6 remedies.
2. Excess acidity / sharp flavor:6 causes,4 remedies. The source places dough liquefaction in the origin column; it is not silently moved into another group.
3. Poor bread development:5 causes,5 remedies.
4. Lack of levain strength:4 causes,4 remedies.

All19 causes and19 remedies are represented, with seven listed symptoms. Presentation is paraphrased; source groups are not converted into19 unsupported one-to-one causal pairings. Main table has Defect / Possible causes / Remedies columns; mobile stacks those cells within each original group.

The source gives8–10h,50%levain and30%levain as examples. It does not specify the percentage denominator in this table, so no batch/dose calculation is added. Its maximum2%commercial-yeast remedy does not identify yeast form or basis here, and “a little salt” has no dose; do not silently supply either. Retain the poor-development remedy above10°C(50°F) in that context, not as a universal storage rule. The ambiguous printed cause “Not enough yeast to make yeast production” is preserved in source notes without inventing a different biological claim. No pH, feeding-ratio or numeric humidity/temperature adjustments invented.
